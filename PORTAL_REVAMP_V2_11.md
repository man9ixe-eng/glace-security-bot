# Glace Staff Hub v2.11

## Trello-linked manual Junior Staff recovery

`/manualjuniorlog` now requires the original Trello session card link.

The command loads the card directly and uses its:

- due date/time for the recovered activity timestamp;
- card title for the original entered time zone and host;
- session type;
- Trello URL and card identifier.

Each recovered Junior Staff log includes a direct link back to the session card. This continues to affect Junior Staff Roblox training activity only and does not modify Leadership Intern+ activity tracking.
