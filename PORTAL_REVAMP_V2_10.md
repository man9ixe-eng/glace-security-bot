# Glace Staff Hub v2.10

## Trello title behavior
Session card titles preserve the source time and timezone entered by the host. The Trello due-date instant is converted separately for the Eastern-configured Trello account.

## Manual Junior Staff log recovery
`/manualjuniorlog` lets Corporate+ recover missed Junior Staff Roblox training logs in bulk. The command posts parser-compatible records into the existing Junior Logs channel and preserves the original session timestamp. It does not write to Leadership Intern+ activity tracking.
